German Robotic CNC Machining

Human variance in aluminium joinery causes whistling drafts, water ingress, and acoustic leakage. At Relinter, every profile is cut, notched, drilled, and crimped by German Elumatec 5-axis robotic machining centers.

Direct CAD-to-CNC Machine Coordinates

Revit .RFA and AutoCAD .DWG drawings are translated directly into robotic toolpaths with zero manual interpolation error.

Epoxy-Crimped Dual-Spindle Mitre Joins

Compound mitres are injected with structural German bicomponent epoxy for lifetime airtight structural cohesion.

AEROSPACE GRADE PURITY

Primary Virgin 6063-T6 Billets

We reject recycled commercial scrap. Every Relinter extrusion is cast solely from virgin primary aluminium to ensure high tensile yield strength and flawless surface anodization.

STRUCTURAL PURITY

215 MPa Yield Strength

34mm PA66 Polyamide Breaks
