One We're Known For

The Masala Dosa.

Batter ground and fermented overnight, spread thin on a well-seasoned tawa until the edges turn lace-crisp, then folded over a potato masala we make fresh every morning. It's the dish most regulars order without looking at the board.

Our Shop
Our Story

Made Here. Served Here.

We're a neighbourhood tiffin shop, nothing more, nothing less. Every morning we make the same idli, dosa, vada and pongal our regulars have come to expect, and serve it fresh, hot and straight to the table at our shop.

No fictional history, no big chain — just a kitchen that starts early and a counter that stays open until the last tiffin of the evening is served.
