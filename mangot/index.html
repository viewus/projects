Know Your Mango

Our Mango Varieties

Every mango has its own character. Here's what goes into your cup.

India · Ratnagiri
Alphonso
Known as the "king of mangoes" — saffron-coloured flesh, rich and creamy with a short but glorious season.
India · Gujarat
Kesar
The "queen of mangoes" — deep golden hue, intensely fragrant with a tangy-sweet balance.
Mexico
Ataulfo
Small, golden and virtually fibre-free — buttery texture with a honeyed, floral sweetness.
Florida, USA
Tommy Atkins
Bold red-blushed skin, firm juicy flesh and a mild sweetness — a reliable everyday favourite.

The Story of Mango

Beyond the Juice

Mango has been cherished for over 4,000 years, originating in South Asia and now grown across the tropics. Fresh mango juice is naturally sweet, aromatic, and enjoyed across cultures worldwide.

Beyond juice, mango offers a range of valuable products. At Mangot, we hand-select ripe fruit at peak season for the richest flavour possible.

🍮
Pulp & Purée
Rich base for desserts and lassi
🫙
Chutney

🍦
Sorbet
Frozen treat from real mango flesh
🌱
Eco Fibre
