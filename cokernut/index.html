The Story of the Coconut

Beyond the Water

Known as Kalpavriksha — "the tree that gives everything" — the coconut palm has been cherished for over 4,000 years across South and Southeast Asia and now grows across the tropics. Every part of the fruit finds a use.

Beyond the water, coconuts give us coir rope from the husk, bowls and charcoal from the shell, and virgin oil from the flesh. At Cokernut, even our leftover husks are biodegradable — a truly sustainable crop.
