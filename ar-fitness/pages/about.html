Our Origin

The AR Fitness
Story.

Founded in 2016 in the heart of Bengaluru, AR Fitness Hub started as a small but fierce training facility with a simple mission: produce real results for real people. What started with 2 coaches and 50 members has grown into one of Karnataka's most respected premium fitness brands.

Our founders — competitive athletes themselves — understood that fitness is not just physical. It's mental, emotional, and deeply personal. They built AR Fitness Hub to reflect that understanding at every level: in the coaching, the programming, the community, and the environment.

Today we serve 500+ active members with a team of 15+ certified coaches, delivering transformation results that speak for themselves. We are not just a gym — we are your fitness partner for life.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision.

🎯
Our Mission

To deliver a world-class fitness experience that empowers every member — regardless of their starting point — to transform their body, strengthen their mind, and build habits that last a lifetime. We are committed to science-based training, expert coaching, and an inclusive community where everyone belongs and everyone progresses.

🔭
Our Vision

To be the most trusted and transformative fitness brand in India — a place where champions are made, where excellence is the standard, and where every member's success story becomes an inspiration for the next. We envision a future where AR Fitness Hub is the benchmark for premium fitness across the country.
