Our Story

Who We Are

AR Home Services was founded with a singular belief: every homeowner deserves professional, reliable, and honest service. What started as a small electrical repair business has grown into a full-spectrum home services company trusted by hundreds of families across the city.

Our team of over 40 certified professionals brings expertise across electrical, plumbing, carpentry, and general home maintenance. We combine technical precision with a customer-first approach that sets us apart from the competition.
