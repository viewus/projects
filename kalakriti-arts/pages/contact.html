Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

We accept single-piece custom orders as well as bulk orders. There is no minimum for custom work. For bulk orders, we start at 20 units.

How long does a custom order take?

Custom pieces typically take 3–6 weeks depending on complexity and size. We'll give you a precise timeline after the design consultation.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, we deliver pan-India and also ship internationally. All pieces are packed with special protective materials to ensure safe transit.

Can I visit the workshop?

Absolutely! Workshop visits are by appointment only. We love having visitors — it's a wonderful way to see the art being made live.
