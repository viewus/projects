Our Story

Born in a Village,
Loved by the World

Kalakriti was born in the dusty lanes of Kondapalli — a small village in Krishna district, Andhra Pradesh, that has quietly produced some of India's most beloved wooden toys for over 400 years. The art form, known as Kondapalli Bommalu, uses the lightweight Tella Poniki wood, hand-carved into vivid scenes of rural life, mythology, and nature.

In 2008, our founder Rajeshwari Devi — a third-generation craftswoman — decided to take her family's art beyond the village and into the hands of people across India and beyond. She started with a handful of dolls, a dream, and decades of inherited technique.

Today, Kalakriti works with over 40 artisan families, ensuring that every craftsperson earns fairly, works with dignity, and carries the tradition forward — one hand-painted stroke at a time.

Our Inspiration

From the Gods, From the Soil

The stories that inspire Kondapalli craft are ancient — the Ramayana, the Mahabharata, the daily rhythms of rural Andhra. You'll find Krishna playing his flute, a bullock cart carrying produce to market, a bride adorned in silk. These aren't just decorative objects — they are cultural archives, preserved in wood and colour.

We also draw from contemporary life — creating modern miniature scenes, abstract interpretations, and nature-inspired collections that speak to today's aesthetics while honouring yesterday's techniques.

Our Values

What We Stand For

🌿

Sustainability

We source only naturally fallen wood and use plant-based dyes — ensuring our craft leaves the earth better than we found it.

🤝

Fair Artisanship

Every artisan we work with receives fair wages, safe working conditions, and recognition for their irreplaceable skill.

🏛️

Cultural Preservation

We actively document, archive, and teach traditional craft techniques so they are never lost to future generations.

✨

Uncompromising Quality

Each piece undergoes rigorous quality review. If it doesn't meet our standards, it goes back to the artisan — never to the customer.

💡

Creative Innovation

We honour tradition while embracing contemporary design sensibilities — creating pieces that feel both timeless and today.

❤️

Community First

10% of every order goes directly into our artisan welfare fund, supporting health, education, and housing in craft villages.
