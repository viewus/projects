How It Works

From Idea to Heirloom

A transparent, personal process that keeps you involved at every stage.

01

Consultation

We begin with a conversation — understanding your vision, purpose, budget, and timeline before a single cut is made.

02

Design Sketch

Our artists create detailed sketches and material samples for your approval. Revisions are always welcome at this stage.

03

Handcrafting

The actual making begins — carving, shaping, painting, drying. You receive progress photos at key milestones.

04

Delivery

Carefully packed in eco-friendly materials, your piece arrives safely — with care instructions and a certificate of authenticity.

Why Kalakriti

Craft with Conscience

We are not a factory. Every order placed with us directly sustains an artisan family. We limit our production volumes intentionally — to ensure quality never slips and our artisans are never overworked.

When you choose Kalakriti, you're not just buying a beautiful object. You're investing in a living tradition, a family's livelihood, and the future of Indian craft.
