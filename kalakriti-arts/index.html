Who We Are

Where Ancient Art
Finds New Life

Kalakriti is a home of authentic handmade crafts rooted in the timeless Kondapalli Bommalu tradition. We believe every object can tell a story — painted in living colour, carved from sacred wood, and gifted with the warmth of human hands.

Our artisans — many of whom are third-generation craftspeople from Andhra Pradesh — pour their heritage into each piece, ensuring that every product you receive is truly one of a kind.

🪵

Tella Poniki Wood

We source the traditional light-weight softwood used for centuries in Kondapalli craft.

🎨

Natural Colour Dyes

All paints are derived from natural sources — tamarind seed paste, vegetable colours, and mineral pigments.

🤲

100% Handmade

No machines. Every curve, every colour, every detail is a product of human skill and devotion.
