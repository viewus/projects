The Cows

It All Starts With A Healthy Cow.

Good milk begins long before collection. It starts with how a cow is fed, how much space she has to graze, and how well she's looked after day to day. Our partner farms are chosen because they take this seriously.

🌾
Open Pasture Grazing
Cows spend time outdoors on open pasture whenever conditions allow, with access to fresh grass and clean water.
🩺
Regular Veterinary Care
Herd health is checked routinely, with veterinary support on hand at each partner farm.
⏰
Calm, Consistent Milking
Cows are milked on a steady daily rhythm, using gentle handling practices that keep the herd relaxed.
🏡
Comfortable Shelter
Clean, well-ventilated barns give the herd shelter and rest away from extreme weather.
