Who We Are

Born from the Soil, Built for Green Living

AR Plants & Seeds began with a single belief: that every home, every office, every corner of human life deserves the quiet dignity of living plants. Founded over 15 years ago by a family of passionate horticulturists, we have grown from a small nursery in Bangalore into one of South India's most trusted plant and seed specialists.

We work directly with growers across Karnataka, Kerala, and Tamil Nadu — ensuring every plant that leaves our care is nurtured, healthy and ready to thrive in its new environment.

Our rare plant collection sources from certified botanical growers across Southeast Asia and South America, bringing the world's most extraordinary species to Indian homes and gardens.

Our Mission

Sustainability at Our Core

Every action we take is guided by a commitment to the planet's future.

♻️

Zero-Waste Packaging

We use 100% biodegradable, recycled or reusable packaging materials. No single-use plastics, ever.

💧

Water-Smart Nursery

Our nurseries use drip irrigation and rainwater harvesting, reducing water usage by 60% compared to conventional nurseries.

🌾

Organic Cultivation

All plants are grown using organic compost and natural pest management — no harmful chemicals, ever.

🌳

Green for Every Sale

For every order we fulfil, we plant one tree in a deforested region of India through our Green Back Initiative.
