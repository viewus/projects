Who We Are

A Workshop Born from a Love of Wood

Artisano began in 2018 when master woodworker Vikram Nair left a corporate career to pursue his lifelong passion — creating furniture that tells a story. Starting with a 600 sq ft workshop in Bengaluru's Indiranagar district, he assembled a small team of artisans who shared his philosophy: furniture should be an heirloom, not a commodity.

Six years on, our workshop has grown to 5,000 sq ft. We've completed over 120 projects across India — from intimate bedroom suites to complete office fit-outs spanning thousands of square feet. But our core belief remains unchanged: every piece deserves the same obsessive attention to detail.

We work exclusively with sustainably sourced hardwoods — teak, walnut, oak, sheesham, and rosewood — selected for their grain, stability, and beauty. Each board is inspected, kiln-dried, and hand-finished by artisans who have spent decades mastering their craft.

Our Journey

Six Years of Growth

From a single workbench to a full-scale studio — every year a new chapter.

Workshop Founded

Vikram Nair and two master craftsmen open a 600 sq ft workshop in Indiranagar, Bengaluru, completing their first bespoke dining table commission.

2018
2019

First Interior Project

Artisano completes its first full interior woodwork project — a luxury villa in Whitefield — earning recognition from Architectural Digest India.

Studio Expansion

We move to a 2,000 sq ft facility and grow our team to 12 artisans. The sofa collection is launched, combining master upholstery with hardwood joinery.

2020
2021

Corporate Division

Launch of Artisano Corporate — dedicated to office interiors, hospitality spaces, and bulk institutional commissions across India.

Pan-India Reach

Projects delivered in Mumbai, Pune, Chennai, Hyderabad and Delhi. Our team grows to 25 craftsmen across carpentry, upholstery and finishing.

2022
2024

Full Design Studio

Artisano opens its 5,000 sq ft flagship studio and showroom. We now offer end-to-end design consultation, 3D rendering, and white-glove delivery nationwide.

Our Process

From Vision to Masterpiece

A transparent, collaborative process ensures your furniture exceeds every expectation.

01

Design Consultation

We begin with an in-depth conversation about your space, lifestyle, aesthetic preferences, and budget. Our designers create detailed sketches and 3D renders before a single board is cut.

02

Material Selection

Together we choose your wood species, grade, grain pattern, and finish. You visit our timber yard to handpick the actual boards that will form your piece — a uniquely Artisano experience.

03

Crafting & Finishing

Master artisans build your piece using traditional joinery — mortise and tenon, dovetail, and hand-cut joints — finished with food-safe oils, lacquers, or custom paints to your specification.
