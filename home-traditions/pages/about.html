Our Story

About Artisana

Where tradition breathes and hands remember

Who We Are

Guardians of a Living Tradition

Artisana began not with a business plan, but with a memory — a grandfather's workshop in Andhra Pradesh where the smell of lacquer and the rhythm of carving tools were the soundtrack of childhood.

We exist to ensure that these crafts — Kondapalli toys, Etikoppaka lacquerware, Dhokra casting, Kalamkari painting — don't become museum exhibits. They belong in living rooms, boardrooms, and gifting boxes.

Every piece we present is made by an artisan whose family has practised the same craft for at least three generations. We are a bridge between ancient skill and modern appreciation.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Authenticity

No imitations, no shortcuts. Every claim we make can be verified by visiting the artisan who made the piece.

Fair Partnership

Artisans receive 60–70% of every sale — far above industry norms. Their prosperity is our purpose.

Cultural Preservation

We actively document and archive craft techniques, ensuring knowledge survives even when masters retire.

Sustainability

Natural materials, plastic-free packaging, and zero-waste workshops are non-negotiable standards.

Our Journey

Milestones in Craft

2008

The First Workshop

Founded in a small studio in Hyderabad with 6 artisan partners and a dream to take Indian craft global.

2012

National Recognition

Received the National Award for Craft Promotion. Expanded to 45 artisan partners across 4 states.

2016

Going Global

First international corporate gifting order — 2,000 pieces shipped to 14 countries for a Fortune 500 company.

2020

Community Impact

Launched the Artisan Welfare Fund, supporting 200+ families through the pandemic and beyond.

2025

Today

240+ artisan partners, 18,000+ pieces crafted, delivered to 32 countries. The journey continues.
