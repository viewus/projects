Our Heritage

Born from the Hands of Masters

Artisana was born in a small workshop tucked between ancient streets, where the scent of sandalwood mingled with the sound of mallet on chisel. Two decades later, that same spirit drives every piece we create.

We work exclusively with master artisans across India — preserving vanishing crafts while giving them a new language for modern spaces.
