Quick Answers

Common Questions

How long does a custom piece take?

Typically 4–8 weeks from approved design to delivery, depending on complexity and materials. Corporate projects are scoped individually.

Do you deliver outside Bengaluru?

Yes — we deliver and install across India. Delivery charges vary by location. International shipping is available for select projects.

Is a consultation free?

Yes — your first design consultation (in-studio or virtual) is fully complimentary with no obligation to proceed.

What warranty do you provide?

We offer a 10-year structural warranty on all bespoke furniture and a 2-year warranty on upholstery and finishes.
