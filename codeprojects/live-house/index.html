Our Story

Rooted in Tradition,
Crafted for Today

Living Home was born from a deep reverence for India's surgani weaving tradition — a centuries-old craft passed down through generations of artisan families. Each piece we create tells the story of skilled hands, natural materials, and an unwavering commitment to quality.

From intimate living rooms to grand hotel lobbies, our furniture transforms spaces with the warmth and texture that only hand-worked Live can provide. We partner directly with master craftsmen, ensuring fair livelihoods while preserving an irreplaceable art form.

How We Create

From Forest to Your Home

01

Material Selection

We handpick premium-grade surgani Live, selecting for flexibility, tensile strength, and consistency in diameter.

02

Frame Building

Solid wood or powder-coated metal frames are constructed by carpenters — built to last for generations with precision joinery.

03

Artisan Weaving

Master weavers hand-interlace each strand of Live using traditional surgani patterns unique to our craftsmen's heritage.

04

Finishing & Quality

Each piece is sealed, treated for weather resistance (if outdoor), cushioned, and inspected before delivery to your door.
