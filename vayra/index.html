MAISON ASSISTANCE

ATELIER INQUIRIES

Essential information regarding private commissions, precious metal certification, and worldwide white-glove transport.

How do I commission a custom bespoke bridal saree? ▼
Our Master Drapers work directly with you at our private lounges in Bengaluru, Mumbai, London, or via 3D Tele-Atelier. You can select warp thread hues, custom zari family crest motifs, and border dimensions. Typical custom weaving requires 8 to 16 weeks.
How is the purity of 24K real gold zari verified? ▼
Every VAYRA creation arrives with a physical lab certification and encrypted NFT provenance card verifying the silver-to-gold purity ratio tested under government assay laboratories.
What does white-glove insured international delivery include? ▼
We partner with specialized fine art couriers providing 100% full-value insurance coverage, climate-controlled transit, and dedicated door-to-door concierge delivery to over 48 countries.
How should handwoven pure silk be preserved across generations? ▼
Store your saree in our provided breathable muslin wraps within the cedarwood box. Refold along different pleat lines once every 6 months and avoid direct naphthalene contact.
